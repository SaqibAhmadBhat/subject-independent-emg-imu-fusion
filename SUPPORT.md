### 2. Frequently Asked Questions
- **Q: Where are the raw SDALLE recordings?**  
  A: Continuous raw SDALLE recordings are published on Zenodo under DOI [10.5281/zenodo.14841611](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14841611). They are not redistributed in this repository due to size.
- **Q: Can I verify the results without downloading the full dataset?**  
  A: Yes! Run `python scripts/verify_release.py`. It runs in <60 seconds on the precomputed test predictions and verification matrices.
